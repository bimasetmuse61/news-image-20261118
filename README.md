# news-image-20261118

Sum up the world news of Wednesday 18 November 2026 in one image: a
newspaper front page, "THE WORLD — WEDNESDAY 18 NOVEMBER 2026".

Stories (all reported on 18 November 2026):

- **Lead — US-Iran trade blows in the Strait of Hormuz.** US jets hit a
  Revolutionary Guards missile site on Qeshm Island after Iranian drones
  targeted a US radar base in Bahrain; Washington said it sank five
  Iranian tankers, Tehran said it hit ten ships and fired missiles at a
  US base in Jordan.
- **Nvidia doubles its revenue** — $115.8 billion in a quarter, up 103%,
  with data-centre sales of $107.6 billion.
- **Ukraine strikes the Kerch Bridge again**, collapsing a road span for
  the second night running, days before a Putin–Trump–Xi summit.
- **Cyclone Akvan closes in** on the Andhra coast, landfall due Friday
  20 November.
- **India and Japan meet in Tokyo** for 2+2 defence and foreign
  minister talks.
- **Also today:** UN warns of more cold deaths in Gaza; COP31 in Antalya
  enters its decisive phase; Robert Pattinson is named the new James
  Bond; a 5.3 quake shakes Romania.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`). The image was drawn
in the build session with a hand-written PNG encoder and bitmap font;
the program is deterministic and makes no network calls.
